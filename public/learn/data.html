AI Product Discovery

Data strategy & AI flywheels for product managers

Data, not the model, is usually what makes an AI product hard to copy. Know which data you have, improve its quality, capture feedback from usage, and be honest about when data really creates a moat.

Every AI feature depends on data: to ground answers (as in RAG), to evaluate quality, and sometimes to fine-tune. Start with an honest inventory: what data do you have, is it accurate, complete and representative, can you access it technically, and are you allowed to use it for this purpose? Data quality problems show up as AI quality problems.

A data flywheel is a loop where usage creates data that makes the product better, which drives more usage. Design it deliberately: capture signals such as accepted suggestions, edits, corrections and ratings, link them to the output they refer to, and feed them into error analysis, evals and improvements. Without that capture step, usage teaches you nothing.

Be careful with the idea of a "data moat". Large volumes of generic data rarely stay an advantage: the value of more data often plateaus, and competitors can buy or generate similar data. Durable advantage comes from data that is unique, fresh and tied to a valuable workflow, where each customer's usage improves the product for everyone. Always respect privacy, consent and contracts when reusing customer data.

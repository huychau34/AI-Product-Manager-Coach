AI Product Discovery

AI feasibility & ROI for product managers

Leadership will ask 'is it worth it?' ROI = value of time/quality gained minus cost per task (inference, review, maintenance). Include human review time and error cost.

AI ROI comes down to value per task minus cost per task, multiplied by volume. Value can be time saved, higher quality, extra revenue or lower risk. Cost includes inference (API tokens), infrastructure, human review, the cost of errors, and the build plus ongoing maintenance - monitoring, evaluation and prompt updates.

Watch the hidden costs. Correction time eats savings: if 30% of AI drafts need heavy fixing, the average saving can be close to zero. Edge cases take a disproportionate share of effort. And unlike traditional software, AI has a variable cost per use, so heavy usage directly affects gross margin and how you should price the feature.

Estimate ROI with a pilot rather than a spreadsheet alone. Baseline today's time and quality, run the AI on a real sample, and measure acceptance rate, correction time, error rate and cost per task. Then test sensitivity: which assumption would flip the business case? Your levers are a cheaper model, narrower scope, caching, and human review only where the risk is high.

Career prep

Interview drill & PRD exercise for product managers

Hiring managers test AI product judgement. Practise structuring answers: problem, users, AI pattern, data, risks, evaluation, metrics.

AI PM interviews usually combine classic product sense with AI judgement. Expect to design an AI feature, critique an existing one, handle a hallucination incident, explain a technical concept simply, and discuss metrics and trade-offs.

A reliable structure: clarify the user and problem, then explain why AI (rather than rules), choose a pattern (copilot, assistant or agent) and the data it needs, name the risks (hallucination, bias, privacy, safety) with mitigations, describe the evaluation plan and launch criteria, and finish with success metrics and how you'd iterate. Interviewers reward clear trade-offs over buzzwords.

An AI PRD adds sections a normal PRD lacks: expected model behaviour with examples of good and bad output, data sources and access, evaluation criteria and thresholds, failure handling and fallback UX, human oversight, a cost-per-task budget, and post-launch monitoring. Writing one for a real idea is excellent interview prep and portfolio material.

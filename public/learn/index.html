AI product management topics

Free guides for product managers moving into AI. Each topic is a lesson in the AI PM Coach roadmap, with practice and AI feedback.

AI Fundamentals

LLMs, tokens & hallucinations
An LLM predicts the next token from context. It has a limited context window and can produce confident but wrong text (hallucination). Product implication: design for verification, not blind trust.Prompt engineering
Good prompts give role, context, task, constraints and output format. Few-shot examples steer style; structured (JSON) output makes results usable by software.

AI Product Discovery

Finding AI opportunities & data readiness
Good AI use cases: fuzzy inputs, tolerance for imperfection, plenty of relevant data, clear value if right 80-90% of the time. Check data quality, access and privacy before committing.AI feasibility & ROI
ROI = value of time/quality gained minus cost per task (inference, review, maintenance). Include human review time and error cost.Data strategy & AI flywheels
Know which data you have, improve its quality, capture feedback from usage, and be honest about when data really creates a moat.

AI Product Design

Copilots, assistants & agents
Copilots suggest inside a workflow; assistants answer on demand; agents take multi-step actions with tools. More autonomy means more risk, so add human-in-the-loop for high-impact steps.Designing AI UX & trust
Good AI UX sets expectations, shows why the AI said something, makes errors easy to spot and fix, and learns from feedback.

RAG & Enterprise AI

Embeddings & RAG
Embeddings turn text into vectors so similar meaning is close. RAG retrieves relevant documents first, then has the LLM answer from them: no retraining, and answers can cite sources.Prompting vs RAG vs fine-tuning
Start with prompting, add RAG when the model needs your knowledge, and fine-tune only to change behaviour or style at scale.

Evaluation, Safety & Operations

AI evaluation
Build an evaluation set of real questions with ground-truth answers. Track accuracy, hallucination rate, acceptance rate, human corrections, latency and cost per task.Responsible AI, safety & compliance
Identify harms early (bias, privacy, misuse), add guardrails and red-teaming, and know which rules, such as the EU AI Act, apply.Launching & monitoring AI features
Roll out gradually, log and trace AI interactions, monitor quality, cost and latency, and have a plan for incidents.

AI Product Strategy

AI strategy, economics & metrics
Decide build vs buy, where your proprietary data creates advantage, and unit economics (cost per task vs. price/value). Pair adoption metrics with quality and cost metrics.Pricing & monetising AI features
Understand AI unit economics, choose between seat, usage and outcome pricing, and protect margins as usage grows.

Career prep

Interview drill & PRD exercise
Practise structuring answers: problem, users, AI pattern, data, risks, evaluation, metrics.

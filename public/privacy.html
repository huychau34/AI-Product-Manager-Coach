Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

AI PM Coach (aipmcoach.netlify.app) is a free learning app that helps product managers build AI product management skills through lessons, practice questions, AI feedback, simulators and a capstone project. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, where it is stored and the choices you have. The app is run by Huy Chau ("we", "us").

1. Using the app without an account

You can use AI PM Coach without signing in. In that case your assessment answers, roadmap, lesson progress, reflections, mentor chats, simulator answers and capstone drafts are saved only in your own browser's local storage. We do not receive a copy. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

2. Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you choose "Continue with Google", we use Google OAuth through our account provider, Supabase, and receive only the following from your Google account:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your Google account identifier (to recognise you when you sign in again)

We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

How we use it: to create your AI PM Coach account, save your learning progress so you can continue on any device, show your name in the app, and let you delete your account.

AI PM Coach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below, who process it only to run the app.

3. Data stored when you have an account

  • Account data: name, email address, sign-up date and last sign-in time.
  • Learning progress: the same progress described in section 1 (assessment answers, roadmap, completed lessons, answers, reflections, chats, simulator results, capstone drafts, achievements and settings), so it can be restored on any device.
  • Usage activity: for each day you use the app, the date, how many times you signed in and when you were last active. We use this to understand how many people use the app (for example, daily active users) and to improve it.

The site owner can see, in a private admin dashboard, each account's name, email address, sign-up date, last activity, number of active days and number of lessons completed.

4. AI features

When you use an AI feature (the AI Mentor, reflection feedback, new practice questions, capstone reviews, role-play and interview simulators), the text you enter, your assessment answers (such as experience level, industry and goal), your optional product description and the current lesson are sent to our server and to Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model, to generate a reply. We do not store these messages on our server, apart from saving them in your progress as described above. Please don't enter confidential or sensitive personal information into AI features.

AI features are available to signed-in learners. To prevent abuse, our server checks your sign-in with Supabase and keeps a daily count of AI requests per account, identified by an anonymised (hashed) account ID.

5. Feedback, videos and analytics

  • Feedback form: if you send feedback, your message, rating, the lesson it concerns and, optionally, your email address are stored with Netlify Forms so we can read and reply to it.
  • Videos: lesson videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube may store data under its own privacy policy.
  • Analytics: if enabled, we use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand how the app is used, only after you give consent in the app. You can change your choice at any time on the in-app Privacy page.

6. Service providers and where data is stored

  • Netlify hosts the website and runs the server functions.
  • Supabase stores accounts, progress and activity data in an EU region.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • Anthropic processes text sent to AI features.
  • YouTube provides embedded videos; PostHog (EU) provides analytics only with consent.

Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA; where they do, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

Account data, progress and activity are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, progress and activity data are deleted from our database. Feedback messages are kept until they are no longer needed to improve the app. Data kept only in your browser stays until you clear it.

8. Your rights and choices

You can at any time:

  • use the app without an account;
  • download a copy of your progress (Progress or Account page → "Download my data");
  • delete your account and all its data (Account page → "Delete my account");
  • sign out, which removes your progress from that browser;
  • withdraw analytics consent.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to a data protection authority (in Finland, the Data Protection Ombudsman, tietosuoja.fi). Our legal bases are your consent (sign-in, analytics) and our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the app.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. The database can only be accessed by our server using a secret key; each request is checked against your Google sign-in, so you can only access your own progress. API keys are never exposed to the browser.

10. Children

AI PM Coach is intended for adults working in, or preparing for, product management. It is not directed at children under 16.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. For questions or requests about your data, contact Huy Chau via the in-app feedback form or on LinkedIn.
